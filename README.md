# Dear Data 100 - How much time do I spend on my phone

## What data am I collecting?
I am collecting data about my daily phone usage, including how much time I spend on my phone, which apps I use the most, how frequently I check my phone, and the number of notifications I receive. 
I am interested in understanding my phone habits and seeing how much of my day is spent on my device.

## How am I collecting data?
I am collecting the data manually by keeping track of my phone usage each day. I will use my phone's built-in screen time settings to record the amount of time spent on different apps, alongside noting how often I check my phone and receive notifications. I will then organise this information into categories so I can identify patterns and visualise my phone habits.
